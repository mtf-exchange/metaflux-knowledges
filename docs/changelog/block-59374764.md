## Backstop funding {#backstop-funding}

Three rules change how the [deficit waterfall](../concepts/tiered-liquidation.md#t4--the-deficit-waterfall) is funded. None changes an `/info` or `/exchange` field.

- **The treasury pays queued debt as fees accrue.** After every protocol-fee split, the treasury balance pays the debt queued on native markets, lowest market id first, until the balance is spent. The treasury balance is the accrued [~10% treasury share](../concepts/fees.md#where-fees-go) of protocol fees. The first split after the height also spent what the treasury already held. Before the height, that debt waited for a multisig treasury draw. A debt on a builder-deployed market stays its own and is never paid from the share.
- **A liquidation fee pays down queued debt at once.** When a forced close credits the insurance fund of a market, the fund pays any debt queued on that same market before it holds the rest. Before the height, the fund held the fee and the debt waited for a treasury draw. The fund of one market never pays the debt of another market. The [capacity cap](../api/rest/info/perpetuals.md#oi-cap-capacity) of the market falls by the amount paid, because it is sized from the fund.
- **A reserve top-up pays from the treasury share.** The system operations that add to the insurance fund or to the treasury reserve pay from the [~10% treasury share](../concepts/fees.md#where-fees-go) of protocol fees. A top-up larger than the treasury balance is refused. Before the height, a top-up added to a reserve and took the value from nowhere. A top-up is accepted only on the USDC token or on a native perp market, because those are the pools that hold USDC.

The multisig draw stays as the manual path.

## Batch deployer price push {#mip3-oracle-batch}

[`mip3_set_oracle_pxs`](../api/rest/exchange/deploy-perp.md#mip3_set_oracle_pxs) (action 232) pushes the index price of 1 to 64 MIP-3 markets under one signature. It is all or nothing, and each entry acts like one [`mip3_set_oracle_px`](../api/rest/exchange/deploy-perp.md#mip3_set_oracle_px). Before the height, the node refused it with `unknown variant`. The single push is unchanged.

Sign it with Rust SDK 0.29.0 or TypeScript SDK 0.33.0, or later. An older SDK cannot sign it.
